# Global English Headline Governance (ACS-SCF Framework — Sovereign Conversion Framework)

All agents MUST follow this governance whenever creating, editing, or evaluating **HEADLINES** for landing pages, technical product announcements, Hacker News posts, Product Hunt submissions, or developer social copy in **English**.

A headline is not meant to sound poetic or clever—it must successfully perform **3 Mandatory Duties of the ACS-SCF Framework** (Sovereign Conversion Framework).

---

## 🎯 1. The Three Mandatory Duties of a Headline (ACS-SCF Framework)

### Duty #1: Stop the Scroll (Attention Interruption — First 2 Seconds)
A headline is the front door. Its primary job is to **make the reader's thumb stop scrolling**:
- If the front door fails to stop the scroll, nobody reads the body copy. If nobody reads, nobody converts.
- Avoid boring, flat statements. Create an immediate visual or intellectual pause.

### Duty #2: Spark Curiosity (Curiosity Resonance)
Stopping the scroll is worthless if the reader bounces a second later. The headline must trigger active curiosity:
- **Success Metric:** The reader thinks: *"Wait, how is that possible?"*, *"That sounds exactly like my problem!"*, or *"Tell me more."*
- Curiosity pulls the reader past distractions and makes them lean forward to read the next sentence.

### Duty #3: Audience Filtering (Precision Targeting)
The headline MUST act as an automatic filter to attract qualified users and filter out unqualified noise:
- Speak directly to software engineers, tech leads, and founders who actually manage AI agents and codebases.
- Filtering saves hundreds of support hours by setting clear, accurate expectations upfront.

---

## ❓ 2. The Power of Question Headlines

When in doubt, prioritize a **Provocative Question Headline** over a flat statement:

- **Psychology:** When someone reads a question, the human brain automatically pauses to search for the answer. It forces focused attention.
- **Conversion Shift:**
  - ❌ Flat Statement: *"ACS prevents AI agents from executing commands in the wrong environment."*
  - ✅ Question: *"Tired of watching your AI agent accidentally test in production? Meet the local guardrail."*

---

## 🛠️ 3. The 17 Battle-Tested Headline Formulas (Global English Edition)

**Variables:**
- **[DESIRED_OUTCOME]:** The tangible, real-world relief or win the user experiences.
- **[PAIN_POINT]:** The annoying, costly, or painful status quo before using the product.
- **[TEDIOUS_WAY]:** The slow, manual, or clunky old way of solving the problem.

---

### Formula #1: We [DO THIS], But with [DESIRED_OUTCOME].
- *Concept:* Customers don't buy features; they buy the difference it makes in their day.
- *Example:* *We built an AI configuration engine, but with zero terminal flashing and instant environment locks.*

### Formula #2: Get [BENEFIT] Without [TEDIOUS_WAY].
- *Concept:* Deliver the positive outcome while removing the dreaded chore.
- *Example:* *Get rock-solid AI agent guardrails without configuring a dozen fragile scripts.*

### Formula #3: Tired of [PAIN_POINT]? But Still Need [DESIRED_OUTCOME]? Try [PRODUCT]!
- *Concept:* Acknowledge developer fatigue before presenting the solution.
- *Example:* *Tired of babysitting AI coding agents? But still need rapid feature delivery? Try ACS Core.*

### Formula #4: Stop [NEGATIVE_CONSEQUENCE] with [PRODUCT].
- *Concept:* Direct, urgent threat elimination.
- *Example:* *Stop accidental production schema overrides with ACS multi-tier isolation.*

### Formula #5: Let [PRODUCT] Give You [DESIRED_OUTCOME] in [TIME_FRAME].
- *Concept:* A concrete, measurable timeline creates instant plausibility.
- *Example:* *Let ACS configure your complete local agent gateway in under 2 minutes.*

### Formula #6: Join [NUMBER] [TARGET_AUDIENCE] Who [DESIRED_OUTCOME].
- *Concept:* Social proof with authentic, specific numbers.
- *Example:* *Join 550+ engineers who run deterministic AI agent workflows with zero prompt bloat.*

### Formula #7: Want [DESIRED_OUTCOME], But Don't Know How?
- *Concept:* An irresistible short question that interrupts scanning.
- *Example:* *Want air-gapped staging for Claude Code, but don't want to manage Docker containers?*

### Formula #8: What [AUTHORITY/EXPERTS] Say About [PAIN_POINT].
- *Concept:* Authority-triggered credibility.
- *Example:* *What Senior DevOps Architects Say About Unconstrained AI Agent Tool Execution.*

### Formula #9: From Today, You Can Have Both [CORE_WIN] and [BONUS_WIN].
- *Concept:* Overcoming a false trade-off.
- *Example:* *From today, you can run 10 autonomous subagents and keep your laptop battery cool.*

### Formula #10: Say Goodbye to [PAIN_POINT], Say Hello to [SOLUTION].
- *Concept:* Crisp, punchy transition from pain to relief.
- *Example:* *Say goodbye to noisy console popups. Say hello to invisible, headless agent daemons.*

### Formula #11: Your Search for [DESIRED_OUTCOME] Ends Here.
- *Concept:* Strong suggestion of finality and comprehensive resolution.
- *Example:* *Your search for a single-binary, local-first AI developer gateway ends here.*

### Formula #12: The Only [CATEGORY] Built Specifically for [AUDIENCE].
- *Concept:* High-focus differentiation.
- *Example:* *The only local system suite built specifically for full-autonomy Claude Code agents.*

### Formula #13: Get the [CORE_BENEFIT] You've Always Wanted.
- *Concept:* Tapping into latent developer desires for order and control.
- *Example:* *Get the deterministic agentic workflow you’ve always wanted.*

### Formula #14: Eliminate [PAIN_POINT]. Once and for All.
- *Concept:* Bold, confident permanence.
- *Example:* *Eliminate cross-environment agent leaks. Once and for all.*

### Formula #15: Who Knew [DESIRED_OUTCOME] Could Be This Simple?
- *Concept:* Surprise and delight at effortless simplicity.
- *Example:* *Who knew air-gapped local AI agent staging could be this fast?*

### Formula #16: We Help [AUDIENCE] Like You [DESIRED_OUTCOME].
- *Concept:* Personal empathy and peer alignment.
- *Example:* *We help solo founders run an entire AI agent engineering team with zero chaos.*

### Formula #17: The Most [DESIRED_ATTRIBUTE] [CATEGORY] Ever Built.
- *Concept:* A bold superlative that challenges the reader to verify.
- *Example:* *The cleanest, lowest-overhead local AI configuration engine ever built.*
