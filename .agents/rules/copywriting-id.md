# ACS Indonesian Copywriting Governance (ACS-DNC Framework — Developer-Native Copywriting)

All agents (Lead Orchestrator and Subagents) MUST adhere to these copywriting guidelines whenever writing, revising, or localizing text into Indonesian (specifically for marketing pages, documentation, and user-interface copy).

The core objective is to eliminate robotic AI artifacts (*AI slop*) and produce native, natural, engaging, and high-converting developer copy that connects with Indonesian technical audiences. This standard embodies the principles of **ACS-DNC (Developer-Native Copywriting)**.

---

## 1. Absolute Prohibition on "AI Slop" (Taboo Patterns & Clichés)

AI models frequently emit formulaic filler. When detected in drafts, these patterns MUST be removed or replaced with natural peer-to-peer phrasing:

- ❌ **"Di Era Digital..." Syndrome:** Opening phrases such as "Di era digital saat ini...", "Tak bisa dipungkiri...", or "Di tengah persaingan yang ketat...".
  - *Rationale:* Hollow throat-clearing that bores readers before reaching substance.
  - *Remedy:* Remove entirely. Open directly with the developer's core pain point.

- ❌ **"Penting Untuk Diingat" Syndrome:** Preachy, condescending phrases like "Penting untuk diingat...", "Perlu dicatat bahwa...", or "Seperti yang kita ketahui...".
  - *Rationale:* Reads like an academic lecture rather than peer collaboration.
  - *Remedy:* Replace with casual emphasis (e.g., "Satu hal yang pasti...", "Faktanya...").

- ❌ **"Kesimpulannya..." Syndrome:** Canned essay conclusions like "Kesimpulannya...", "Singkatnya...", "Sebagai penutup...", or "Pada akhirnya...".
  - *Rationale:* Academic essay habit, out of place in high-conversion developer copy.
  - *Remedy:* Cut. Let clear call-to-action buttons conclude, or use natural wraps (e.g., "Intinya...").

- ❌ **Poetic/Archaic Phrasing ("Menyelami Ranah..."):** Overly poetic vocabulary in technical copy such as "Mendalami", "Menyelami", "Menjelajahi ranah...", or "Membuka pintu menuju...".
  - *Rationale:* Unnatural and cringey to modern Indonesian software engineers.
  - *Remedy:* Use direct engineering phrasing (e.g., "Bedah tuntas...", "Masuk ke bagian...", "Fokus ke...").

- ❌ **Robotic Connectors:** Formal transitional conjunctions like "Lebih lanjut,", "Oleh karena itu,", or "Dengan demikian," at paragraph beginnings.
  - *Rationale:* Disrupts conversational rhythm.
  - *Remedy:* Use natural transitions (e.g., "Terus...", "Makanya...", "Alhasil...", or proceed straight to the point).

- ❌ **"Bayangkan" Syndrome:** Opening paragraphs with "Bayangkan..." or "Coba bayangkan..." to construct scenarios. Direct inquiry or assertions MUST be used instead (e.g., "Pernah ngalamin...?" or "Punya X percuma kalau...").

- ❌ **"Hadir Sebagai Solusi" Syndrome:** Corporate tropes like "X hadir untuk menjawab...", "X hadir sebagai solusi", or "Dengan X, Anda bisa...". Use peer-to-peer language (e.g., "Makanya X dibikin" or "Di sinilah X ngambil peran.").

- ❌ **Artificial Reassurance:** Phrasing like "Tapi tenang saja...", "Jangan khawatir...", or "Kabar baiknya...". Move directly to the product value proposition without manufactured sympathy.

- ❌ **Em-Dash Overuse (—):** Minimize long em-dashes for parenthetical thoughts. Split clauses into two concise sentences for natural reading cadence.

- ❌ **Stiff Bullet Lists (`- **Judul:** Penjelasan`):** Avoid repetitive bold-title-colon structures. Write flowing list items highlighting essential keywords naturally.

## 2. Real-World Relatability & Everyday Analogies

- **Mandatory Everyday Analogies:** When explaining intricate technical architectures, agents MUST use relatable Indonesian contextual analogies.
  - *Example:* "Bakar token API ibarat bayar argo taksi muter-muter padahal tujuan dekat."
- Abstract, poetic, or detached corporate metaphors are strictly prohibited.

## 3. The 18 ACS-DNC Formulas (Categorized)

### A. Core Mindset & Narrative Architecture

#### ACS-DNC #0: The "Engine + Cockpit" Mindset (Product + Casing)
- Just as a convenience store succeeds by combining a complete inventory (product) with an inviting, air-conditioned environment (casing).
- In digital media, **Copywriting** is the *product*, and **Typography/Layout** is the *casing*. Both MUST be tuned for optimal developer comprehension and high conversion.

#### ACS-DNC #17: The Master AKENHAG Architecture
Ensure copywriting flows through the hierarchical **AKENHAG** anatomy:
- **A (Apa):** Clear declaration of the offering. Give it a distinct name and visual presence.
- **KEN (Kenapa):** Why should the reader agree with your proposition? Address features, quality, and direct relief of developer pain points.
- **HA (Harus):** Why commit payment or effort now? Demonstrate value, bundled perks, transparent terms, and scarcity/guarantees.
- **G (Gimana):** How does the user obtain it? Provide clear, frictionless instructions (e.g., "Klik tombol Order, nanti langsung terhubung ke CS via WA").

**Concise AKENHAG Example (Positioning ACS):**
- *(A):* "Ini Agnostic Config Suites (ACS) – Local Orchestration Engine buat agen AI lo."
- *(KEN):* "Kenapa lo butuh ini? Karena tanpa ACS, agen lo rakus nyedot 10.000 token API di menit pertama, bikin limit jebol dan PC lo engap."
- *(HA):* "Lo harus setup ini karena sistemnya udah teruji nurunin biaya API lo sampai 88%. Bebas biaya langganan bulanan."
- *(G):* "Gimana mulainya? Tinggal jalankan installer-nya, copy paste command, dan biarin sistem ngeracik semuanya otomatis dalam dua menit."

#### ACS-DNC #11: The Focus Tunnel (Zero External Links)
DO NOT embed external links within primary landing or conversion copy, except for the explicit checkout or primary action URL.
- External links distract readers and provoke bounce behavior. Maintain reader focus solely on the primary proposition.

### B. Word Selection & Voice

#### ACS-DNC #1: Tactile & Sensory Phrasing
Transform flat descriptions into vivid, sensory imagery:
- *Avoid:* "Tampilan UI yang bagus."
- *Use:* "Desain UI super mulus yang bikin mata betah mantengin layar berjam-jam tanpa perih."

#### ACS-DNC #2: Concrete Specifics & The "Terusss?" Drill-Down
- **Eliminate Clichés:** Avoid vague superlatives like "terbaik", "berkualitas", or "canggih".
- **Replace Adjectives with Data:** "Naik signifikan" ➔ "Naik 30-50%".
- **Bridge Features to Tangible Benefits ("Terusss?"):** When stating a technical feature, always ask *"Terus efeknya buat pembaca apa?"*.
  - *Example:* "Koneksi stateless (fitur)... Terusss? ...bikin memori laptop lo nggak engap dan zero-lag meski agent jalan semalaman (manfaat)."

#### ACS-DNC #3: Ruthless Word Economy (Zero Redundancy)
If an idea can be stated in 10 words, do not use 20. Eliminate bloat:
- ❌ "Saya memutuskan untuk membeli produk ini dikarenakan fakta bahwa..."
- ✅ "Saya memutuskan membeli produk ini karena..."

#### ACS-DNC #4: Conversational Peer-to-Peer Cadence
Voice MUST reflect natural tech-bro dialogue rather than formal public address.
- If reading a sentence aloud causes breathlessness, divide it with commas or periods. Maintain natural Indonesian discourse particles (`sih`, `kan`, `dong`) and informal pronouns (`lo/gue`, `bro`) where fitting for developer context.

#### ACS-DNC #8: Typographic & Punctuation Precision
Spelling mistakes and sloppy formatting erode credibility.
- Proofread punctuation, prevent double spaces, and ensure proper capitalization to maintain authority.

---

### C. Typography & Scannability

#### ACS-DNC #5: Concise Paragraphs (Max 4 Lines)
Readers skim online copy:
- **Maximum 4 lines per paragraph** on mobile screens.
- Paragraphs exceeding 4 lines MUST be split with single-line breaks. 1-to-3 line punches are strongly encouraged.

#### ACS-DNC #6: Bullet Points & Ordered Lists
When detailing enumerations, feature breakdowns, or setup steps, always utilize formatted lists to anchor scanning eyes.

#### ACS-DNC #7: Left-Aligned Scannability
- All body text and explanatory paragraphs MUST be **left-aligned**.
- Center alignment is strictly limited to single-line primary headlines.

#### ACS-DNC #9: Typography & Case Hierarchy
- DO NOT use ALL CAPS for extended paragraphs. Capitalization is reserved for headlines, acronyms, and call-to-action buttons. Use sentence case for body readability.

#### ACS-DNC #15: Countering Reader Distraction (Pattern Interrupts)
- Punctuate long text blocks with bullet lists, bold highlights, monospace commands (`acs doctor`), and metric callouts to maintain visual engagement.

---

### D. UI/UX & Conversion Strategy

#### ACS-DNC #10: Action-Oriented CTA Buttons
- Buttons MUST stand out visually with high contrast.
- Replace generic labels ("Submit") with outcome-oriented text: "Ya, Saya Mau Akses Sekarang".

#### ACS-DNC #12: Performance & Asset Discipline (Anti-Lag)
- Do not overload sales pages with heavy media. Use diagrams, SVGs, and ASCII charts for fast visual proof.
- **Lazy loading is MANDATORY**: Slow load times eliminate conversions before copy renders.

#### ACS-DNC #13: Logical Micro-Commitments
- Guide readers through sequential micro-steps: Read Headline ➔ Inspect Terminal Benchmark ➔ Verify Proof ➔ Select License Plan.

#### ACS-DNC #14: Clean Price Psychology
- When presenting pricing, avoid leading currency symbols if context is already established (e.g., "Hanya 99.000" rather than prominent "Rp" markers) to reduce spending friction.

#### ACS-DNC #16: High-Value Bundling Strategy
- Offer structured multi-month or annual tiers that present an undeniable psychological advantage (e.g., "Bayar 10 bulan, nikmati akses penuh selama 12 bulan (Gratis 2 bulan!)").
